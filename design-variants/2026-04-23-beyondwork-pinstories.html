Beyond Work

Get to know me outside of work.

I travel because it keeps showing me what's possible — in work, in people, in me. 16 countries, 32 cities, and a handful of trips that actually changed how I build things.

Travel is fatal to prejudice, bigotry, and narrow-mindedness. — Mark Twain

01Mexico City · 2025

🇲🇽 CDMX

Life design

I lived in Mexico City for two months. On the first week I met a friend from Tennessee who'd already been there a year — seeing him live there, not tour it, reframed what a life could look like.

The default 9-to-5 isn't the only way. I knew that in theory. Living it for sixty days rewrote it into instinct.

02Colombia · first solo trip

🇨🇴 Colombia

Empathy as input

My first real solo trip. Met people from every continent; learned what they were each running toward.

The best ideas in product often come from watching an adjacent world. Empathy — the PM skill I care about most — compounds when you put yourself somewhere you don't belong.

03Japan · 2024

🇯🇵 Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka

Operational excellence

A 7-Eleven in Tokyo is better-run than most US restaurants. Once you see that, you can't un-see it — it becomes a new bar for what "great" can mean at an operational level.

I went with Dennis, a friend since I was five. He introduced me to Steve on that trip. Steve and I meet every month now. Trips give you more than ideas.
