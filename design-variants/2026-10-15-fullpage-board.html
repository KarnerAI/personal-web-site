Full-page redesign: 4 directions

Same content and sections in every variant: Hero, Career, Entrepreneurship, Beyond Work, Education. Each uses a different type system, palette and layout on purpose. Scroll inside each frame to see the Career animation fire; open standalone for the real thing at full width and on a phone.

A BroadsheetB Transit AuthorityC Gallery PrintD Departure Board
Variant A
Broadsheet: magazine profile, Fraunces + Instrument Sans, warm paper
Editorial spreads with pull quotes and hairlines, no cards. The only transit element is the Career line, which draws itself on scroll with a marker riding it. Ventures as four equal tear sheets; hobbies as a contact-sheet strip.
Open standalone ↗
Variant B
Transit Authority: full wayfinding system, Manrope + JetBrains Mono, one line color per section
A sticky route-map rail shows where you are on the page. Career is the main line with interchange rings; ventures are a cobalt branch line; hobbies are exit signs; education is the amber terminus. The route draws itself when Career enters view.
Open standalone ↗
Variant C
Gallery Print: Swiss poster grid, Archivo, black on white with red for the numbers
Numbered sections on a strict modular grid; the big outcomes ($14M, 10x, 6,500) set at poster size and counting up once. Career is a Vignelli-style vertical strip map; ventures are a dense index table that expands row by row.
Open standalone ↗
Variant D
Departure Board: dark concourse, IBM Plex, amber and vermillion signals (the wildcard)
Every section is a board. Career is a split-flap departure board whose characters flip into place as it scrolls into view; ventures are an arrivals board with SHIPPED / ACTIVE flags; hobbies are platform tiles.
